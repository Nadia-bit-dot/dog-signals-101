# Cross-source synthesis — reviewed 2026-11-23

## Shared conclusions
- **Context before labels:** S01, S02, S04 and S05 all support considering combinations of signals rather than one behavior alone.
- **Tail movement is not a happiness detector:** S01 and S02 both warn against that shortcut.
- **Stress clues overlap with ordinary behavior:** S01 and S05 emphasize context; S02 adds safety-oriented examples.
- **Sounds need visual context:** S04 offers examples; S02 and S05 provide relevant cautionary context.

## Differences in emphasis, not established contradictions
- S02 focuses on bite prevention and interaction safety; S04 focuses on vocal communication; S05 focuses on stress signs. Different scope should not be mistaken for contradiction.
