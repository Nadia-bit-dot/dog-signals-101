# S05 — AKC Stress Signals

[Original](https://www.akc.org/expert-advice/training/how-to-tell-if-your-dog-is-stressed/) · PDF pp.1–5 · reviewed 2026-11-23.

- pp.2–3: Vocalizations, lip-licking, yawning, tucked tail, pinned ears, visible eye whites and avoidance may be clues to stress when assessed together.
- p.4: Freezing or stiffness can signal that a dog is overwhelmed; pacing also has multiple possible explanations depending on the situation.
- p.5: Identifying and reducing a stressor is a primary response; professional help can be useful if triggers cannot be managed.

**Cross-check:** Reinforces S01's context-first principle and S02's caution against interpreting a single signal in isolation.
