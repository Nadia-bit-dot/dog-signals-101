# S04 — VCA Doggy Dialogue

[Original](https://vcahospitals.com/resources/behavior-dog/communication/decoding-doggy-dialogue) · PDF p.1 · reviewed 2026-11-23.

- Barking has multiple possible functions; surrounding posture and context change its interpretation.
- Whining may occur in attention-seeking, discomfort or social contexts.
- Howling can occur in response to environmental sounds and other situations.
- Growling can occur in play as well as discomfort or warning contexts; the article advises evaluating the situation rather than judging sound alone.

**Scope note:** Introductory educational resource, not an acoustic diagnosis guide.
