# S02 — ASPCA Dog Bite Prevention

[Original](https://www.aspca.org/pet-care/dog-care/dog-bite-prevention) · PDF pp. 1–3 · reviewed 2026-11-23.

- p.1: Stiff posture, forward-facing ears, direct staring, growling or barking can appear together in threatening displays; a raised or moving tail does not automatically mean friendliness.
- p.1: Crouching, lowered head, ears flattened back, lip-licking, yawning, looking away, or withdrawing can occur in anxious or fearful dogs.
- p.2: Mixed signals are possible. Respect a dog's reluctance to interact and supervise interactions with children.
- p.3: Serious or repeated aggressive behavior warrants qualified professional assistance.

**Interpretation guardrail:** These are contextual combinations, not a deterministic emotion classifier.
