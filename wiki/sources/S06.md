## Grounded observations
- A dog's behavior can be affected by several emotional states, including excitement, fear, anxiety and frustration. Approach and avoidance may coexist in a conflicting situation. (pp. 6–7)
- Subtle changes and the overall body posture should be assessed together rather than assigning a single fixed emotional label. (p. 7)
- For an overwhelmed dog, reducing exposure, increasing distance, avoiding forced greetings and allowing recovery in a calm setting are proposed support strategies. (p. 5)
- The source recommends gradual, dog-paced introductions, a predictable routine and opportunities for calm, safe retreat. (pp. 8–10)
- Changes in behavior, persistent fear or unusual restlessness warrant veterinary assessment to consider physical causes. (p. 13)
