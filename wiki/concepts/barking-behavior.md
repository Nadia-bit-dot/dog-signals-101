# Barking and vocal communication
Barking, whining, howling and growling have different possible meanings depending on the situation and accompanying body language. A growl is not always an attack signal; it may also appear in play. [S04](../sources/S04.md).

Barking or growling alongside a stiff posture and threatening approach calls for caution. [S02](../sources/S02.md). Barking can also accompany stress. [S05](../sources/S05.md).
