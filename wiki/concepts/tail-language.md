# Tail language
A wagging tail alone does not establish happiness. Consider tail carriage, stiffness, movement and the rest of the dog's body. [S01](../sources/S01.md) [S02](../sources/S02.md).

A high or moving tail can coexist with other signs of threat or tension, so never use it as a single friendly/unfriendly switch. [S02](../sources/S02.md).
