# Ears and eyes
Pinned-back ears, looking away and visible eye whites may appear among signs of stress or fear. Read them with posture and situation rather than treating them as proof of one emotion. [S02](../sources/S02.md) [S05](../sources/S05.md).

Eye contact and facial tension also require contextual interpretation. [S01](../sources/S01.md).

Related: [Stress](stress-signals.md) · [Context](context-and-interpretation.md).

## Cross-link to whole-body assessment
Individual facial and ear signals should not be interpreted alone; the Dogs Trust source adds a general whole-body and context-first approach, without providing new detailed ear-position classifications in the captured visible text. [S06](../sources/S06.md) (p. 7).
